Physical-play reference

Building an attack

An attack starts at 1 AP and 2 damage potential. Each point of potential is one contest and can cause one wound. Prefixes modify that same attack and normally cost another AP each.

    The damage chain

    Potential is a ceiling, not automatic damage: one potential means one contest and at most one wound. The chain stops as soon as the defender wins or ties.

    Contest 1 · Accuracy vs DEX Dodge overcome
    ↓ success
    Queue wound 1
    ↓
    Contest 2 · Accuracy or Impact vs CON Brace overcome
    ↓ success
    Queue wound 2
    ↓
    Contest 3 · relevant defense Defender wins / ties
    STOP · result: 2 pending wounds
    Impact

    After an attack connects, use the weapon's Impact instead of the retained Accuracy result only when Impact is higher. A high Accuracy roll remains dangerous throughout the chain; there are no critical hits.

    Example: Accuracy 12 with an Impact 8 weapon keeps 12. Accuracy 5 with that weapon uses Impact 8 after the Dodge contest.

    Keep in mind

    Important rules

    • Each point of damage potential is one contest and can cause one wound.
    • Ties favor the defender and stop the damage chain.
    • Losing a melee accuracy contest causes 1 chip damage.
    • Normally one attack cannot wound the same attribute twice.
    • Precision allows one attribute to be wounded twice by that attack.
    • After Dodge, use Impact instead of Accuracy only when Impact is higher.
    • The wounded attribute follows the injury fiction; it is not automatically the defense that failed.
    • Queue wounds during the round; apply all attribute reductions when the round ends.
    • Prefixes cost 1 AP each. Quick is intentionally omitted until its rule is defined.
