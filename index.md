# Word List
Each word belongs to one of the categories:
Source Words, Shaping Words, Mastery Words.

Each word consumes 1 Action Point, and often, but not always, 1 Mana Point.

Spells that consume 3 or more Energy can fail during casting.
DC for that check is Easy (4).
Each additional point of Energy used, increases Casting Difficulty by 1.

---
## Source Words
Barely usable on their own, these word cojure and materialize energy to be used in further casting.
User can add simple somatic gesture such as touch to deliver conjured energy without Shaping Words.

---

## Mastery Words
These are the only words that might not have their own Energy Cost,
but, they require other spell words that provide that Energy.
They stabilize other words, or guarantee degrees of success when casting.

---
### Negation
    Nushik, Spyion
    Components: Verbal, 1 Mana, Somatic

---
### Power Word
    Dovinus
    Components: Verbal, 1 Mana
    Any spell used with Dovinus becomes Verbal only. Affects anything that can hear you.
    If mixed with other Shaping Words, Dovinus Spell delivers these new shapes to those that can hear,
    in any way possible. Additionally, boosts tier of the power die by 1.

---
### Focus Word
    Balrurg
    Components: Verbal, 1 Mana
    Gives advantage on a Spell Casting Check.

---
### Enhancement
    Mabufa
    Components: Verbal, 1 Mana, Somatic
    Versatile word, that has many uses by trasforming Mana and Energies used during casting.
    Can be shaped with optional Somatic Component into:
    - Grant 1 Mana to target (Energy Transfer)
    - Increase any Attribute Die by 1 on the target (Heal & Buff in one)

---
### Ascended Energy
    Divinarius
    Components: Verbal, 1 Mana
    Boosts casting guaranteed success difficulty by one Tier.

---
### Hold Still
    Kurwa, Ja Pierdolę
    Components: Verbal only
    Allows user to hold and stabilize energy of a Spell, before releasing.
    Using Hold Still at least once, in a Spell that uses 3 or more energy,
    reduces Casting Difficulty by 1 tier.
